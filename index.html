Our Mandate

What We Do

Three pillars, one goal: making MRIS a school where every student is heard, included, and proud to show up.

Student Advocacy

We take your concerns straight to the administration, no filter, no delay. Uniform policy, exam stress, cafeteria food, timetable clashes: if it affects students, it is our problem to solve.

Events and Activities

Spirit weeks, cultural nights, sports days, charity drives. We plan the moments that turn a school into a community. Every event is student designed and student run.

Community Building

We connect students across grades, clubs, and interests. Because a stronger school starts with actually knowing the people you walk past every day.

About MRIS Council

The MRIS Student Council is elected by the student body to represent your interests, run school events, and lead community service across all grades. Our volunteering programme is the largest part of what we do, and this portal exists so every hour you give back is recorded properly and counted toward your service award.
